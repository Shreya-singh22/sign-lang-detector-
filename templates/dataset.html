About the Dataset

The ASL Alphabet dataset contains 87,000 images of American Sign Language hand gestures, covering the 26 letters (A–Z) plus Delete, Nothing, and Space — 29 classes total. Each class has 3,000 training images captured at 200×200 pixels under varying lighting and hand positions.

Our model was trained on this dataset using MediaPipe hand landmarks + a Random Forest classifier, achieving real-time inference on live webcam input. Click any letter card below to browse sample images.
